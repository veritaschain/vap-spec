# Commercial contract disclosure correction

**24 October 2026 (JST)**

The earlier statement that VeritasChain Co., Ltd. held ten paid service contracts
with European organizations as of September 2026 has not been substantiated
through reconciliation with primary records. The associated effective-date and
service-sector assertions have not been substantiated by that reconciliation
either. We withdraw this numerical commercial claim from current public
materials pending verification. It must not be used as evidence of sales traction.

This is **not a finding that there were no contracts**. Failure to locate or
reconcile records does not establish nonexistence. This correction asserts no
replacement contract count, customer count, revenue or receipt of payment.

Before any figure is republished, the supporting record must identify the legal
contracting entity, execution/acceptance date, service supplied, paid terms,
status at a precisely stated cutoff date, and counting rule. Distinguish contracts
from unique customers, execution from payment received, and VAP/VCP-related
services from other businesses. Quotes, prospect lists and unsigned drafts alone
do not substantiate an executed paid contract. Avoid counting amendments,
renewals or multiple documents twice unless the disclosed counting rule requires it.

Commercial records do not establish external implementation, independent
validation, acceptance of an Evidence Pack, or protocol adoption.

This dated correction applies to current descriptions. The previously distributed
kit `b1af0dc`, subject `c8ccf97`, source bundle, release assets and historical
execution evidence are not rewritten. Any conflicting commercial statement in
those preserved historical sources should be read with this correction. No
normative specification, verifier, gate, OS experiment or K3 condition changes.
