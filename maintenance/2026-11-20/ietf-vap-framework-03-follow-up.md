# VAP Internet-Draft -03 publication follow-up

**Recorded:** 20 November 2026 (JST). **Status:** informative publication record.

The IETF Datatracker records publication of
[draft-kamimura-vap-framework-03][vap03] on **15 November 2026**.
Its [revision history][history] records that date, and the published document
remains an individual Internet-Draft intended for discussion. Publication does
not constitute IETF Working Group adoption or endorsement.

## Relationship to the 11 November proposal

The [11 November maintenance note][proposal] proposed an explanation of the
relationship between the Internet-Draft's three conceptual groups and the
separate VSO specification's five mandatory layers. It also proposed updating
the VCP reference and refreshing the fixed VSO source reference.

The following items are present in the published -03 document:

- Section 4.1 describes Integrity, Provenance and Accountability as conceptual
  groups and includes a table mapping the five VSO specification layers to
  that overview. Traceability and Domain Profiles remain represented.
- The change history states that no framework requirement is added or changed.
- The companion VCP reference identifies `draft-kamimura-scitt-vcp-05`,
  dated 24 October 2026.
- The VSO source reference identifies VAP v1.2.0, Draft 3, at commit
  `1ce71a66f55d9a20728213b7c3a8bb79270ae579`.

These observations establish correspondence between the proposal's subjects
and the later publication. They do not establish that the maintenance note
was submitted verbatim, identify the submission workflow, or record a VSO
normative adoption decision.

## Reading the historical record

The 11 November note describes its own preparation date and work performed.
Its statement that -02 was the published document, and that the note itself
performed no source editing, rebuilding or submission, is retained as that
dated record. The fixed [published -02 text][vap02] is available for comparison;
the note's unversioned Datatracker link follows later revisions.

The repository README now gives the dated -03 publication update separately
from the 25 October snapshot of companion drafts. This follow-up changes no
