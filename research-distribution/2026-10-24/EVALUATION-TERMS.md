# Proposed evaluation terms — fixed research kit

These are proposed operating terms to confirm once with an evaluator before a
run. Publishing this note does not establish anyone's agreement, independence,
payment entitlement, specification adoption or certification. Use the existing
[reporting instructions](https://github.com/veritaschain/vap-spec/blob/b1af0dc9d368b466bf74e7c1b2a6808fb41949ae/reproduction/os-isolation/REPORTING.md)
and evaluator-report template; no new machine-report format is introduced.

## Scope to agree

- Kit: `b1af0dc9d368b466bf74e7c1b2a6808fb41949ae`; reproduced implementation:
  `c8ccf972196a16d11429a478f4918a08fe27bb73`. Record the downloaded ZIP's SHA-256.
- The evaluator provisions and controls a fresh supported Linux environment and
  performs a new execution. Rechecking VSO's saved outputs is a different activity.
- Report success, mismatches, inability to execute, incomplete attempts and all
  environment/dependency differences. Keep original outputs for every attempt.
- Record the executor, environment controller, relationship to implementers,
  relevant financial/contractual relationships and any VSO assistance. Describe
  assisted external execution separately from independently performed execution.
- If an honorarium is separately agreed, it must not depend on successful tests,
  a favorable conclusion, permission to publish, or favorable attribution. This
  note offers no fee and creates no payment commitment.

## Separate consent decisions

Record the evaluator's answer and source message for each item. A blank answer
means **not agreed**, not permission. Preserve any restrictions or conditions.

| Decision | What must be confirmed |
| --- | --- |
| Execution and report | Acceptance of the fixed scope, failure reporting and assistance disclosure above. |
| Report/results publication | Whether VSO may publish the report, excerpts and conclusions, and which versions. |
| Logs/evidence publication | Whether complete raw logs and evidence may be published; identify exclusions or redactions separately. |
| Personal attribution | Whether VSO may publish the executor/reviewer's name and the exact form of attribution. |
| Organization attribution | Whether VSO may publish the organization name and the authorized wording. |

Participation or delivery of a report does not grant publication or attribution
rights. Before publication, confirm the concrete public text, files/redactions
and attribution with the evaluator. Keep private originals and a separate public
copy with a redaction/change record. A redacted copy must not be represented as
the original checksummed evidence or passed off as a complete machine-verifiable
record. Organization attribution is not an endorsement or certification.

## Recording and decisions

Keep the consent correspondence and review notes outside the checksummed output
directory, alongside the existing evaluator report. Leave machine output
`independence: not_assessed` and `independent_reproduction: not_established`
unchanged. Assess independence separately using the actual relationship,
environment control, executor and support record. Apply the
[result-handling rules](RESULT-HANDLING.md) to each distinct execution.
